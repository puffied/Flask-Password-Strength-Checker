The Optimal PasswordClick here to see more
  • Length: Use at least 12-16 characters.
  • Complexity: Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
  • Unpredictable: Avoid names, dates, or common words.
  • Unique: Never reuse the same password across accounts.

Tip: Combine 3-4 random words with numbers and symbols (e.g., BlueCoffeeTable!42).
